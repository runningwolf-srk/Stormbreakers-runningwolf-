I. THE BREAKING — RunningWolf Blade (Hebrews 4:12)

I don't know exactly when this happened, but I was ash in the tundra.
I was bone in the storm.

Snow on the shield. Salt on the wood.
We came from the fjord — but You called us good.

What happened was I was carrying a dead heart where nobody could see.
Cold war god. Carved-out lie. Iron and pride as armor.

I started believing I had to stay hard to survive.

Looking back now, I can see Your breath hit the valley.
And my dead heart was warm.
You split the night and opened the door.

So I lay down iron. I lay down pride.
Your name goes first — now and forevermore.

Lyric witness: "I was ash in the tundra / I was bone in the storm / Then Your breath hit the valley / And my dead heart was warm"

Hebrews 4:12 — For the word of God is alive and active. Sharper than any double-edged sword.

The Word guides the blade.

---

II. THE SEARCHING — Deep in the pine where the ravens flew

Deep in the pine where the ravens flew, we heard Your mercy old and new.
I searched through identity, music, strength, purpose — through fjord and lowlands.

What I was trying to find was a place where mercy was both old and new at once.

---

III. THE ENCOUNTER — The Word cutting through

O King of Heaven, hear our cry.
Fire in the lowlands, light in the sky.

Your Word split the night.
Your cross lifted up high — not a cold war god, not a carved-out lie.

---

IV. THE KNEELING — Lord of Lords (Revelation 19:16)

The mountains tremble at Your voice.
The seas grow still before Your throne.
The nations rise, the kingdoms fall,
Yet You remain forever known.

I am not the King. I thought war horns and banners would save me.
But victory was Yours before the fight.

No crown of earth can match Your glory.
No throne can stand beside Your name.

So the Keeper kneels.

The cross became the conqueror's crown.
The empty grave became our light.
The battle ends beneath Your feet.

When the Keeper kneels, the King stands.

Lyric witness: "No crown of earth can match Your glory. No throne can stand beside Your name."

Revelation 19:16 — On his robe and on his thigh he has this name written: KING OF KINGS AND LORD OF LORDS.

Every knee will bow. Every tongue confess.

---

V. THE BLOOD — Blood of Cross (Colossians 1:20)

On the hill… He bore the weight… The cross… the cost… the love…

He walked the road in silent pain, carrying love through wind and rain.
The Son of God, He did not fall — He gave His life, He gave it all.

Earth was shaking, sky grew dark, yet love ignited every heart.

Peace was not won by the blade. It was bought by blood.

Blood of the cross, it speaks for me — breaking chains and setting free.
Love stood strong where darkness fell — mercy broke the gates of hell.

I am free… I am free… Chains are gone… I believe…
I am healed… I am free… Love has won over me…

Through His sacrifice we rise. Heaven opened in our lives.
Every drop, every cost — victory in the blood of the cross.

Lyric witness: "Blood of the cross, it speaks for me / Breaking chains and setting free"

Colossians 1:20 — Through him to reconcile to himself all things, by making peace through his blood, shed on the cross.

It is finished… It is done… Love remains… The victory won…

---

VI. THE BECOMING — Why the Keeper

We bow in the thunder. We kneel in the flame.
One people, one promise, one conquering name.

I didn't become the Keeper to be above the story.
I became the Keeper because I was ash carried to breath.

The King gives the Word. The Word writes the Chronicle.
The Chronicle is carried by the Keeper. The Keeper unveils the Relics.

So I rise, I rise — with my hands to the throne.
All the old chains falling. All the old fear gone.

From the deep to the heights, let Your kingdom remain.

Jesus, lead us on.
